# Privacy Policy for DateGrab – Web to Google Calendar

**Publisher:** CH5 Labs  
**Last updated:** November 17, 2026

## 1. Overview
DateGrab ("the Extension") is a browser extension developed by CH5 Labs to help users extract upcoming dates, times, and event links from webpages and create Google Calendar links. Your privacy is important to us, and this extension is built on a strict local-first principle.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage
* **No Personal Data Collected:** DateGrab does not collect, store, track, or transmit any personally identifiable information, browsing history, or usage analytics to CH5 Labs or any third-party analytics servers.
* **Local API Key Storage:** To use DateGrab, you provide your own Google Gemini API key. This key is stored strictly locally on your device using your browser's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API and is never transmitted to CH5 Labs.

## 3. How Webpage Data is Processed
* **User-Initiated Action Only:** DateGrab does not read or monitor webpages in the background. Text and links from the active tab are accessed only when you explicitly click the DateGrab toolbar icon.
* **Third-Party AI Processing:** When you trigger a scan, the extracted text and relevant links from the active webpage are sent directly from your browser to Google's Generative Language API (`://googleapis.com`) using your personal API key solely for the purpose of identifying event dates and details.
* **Google Calendar Links:** Clicking "+ Add to Google Calendar" opens a standard pre-filled Google Calendar template URL in a new tab. No direct access to your Google Calendar account is requested or granted.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Your use of the Google Gemini API is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google APIs Terms of Service. CH5 Labs has no control over, and assumes no responsibility for, Google's data handling practices.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
CH5 Labs may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to the extension or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

## 6. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the DateGrab extension, please contact CH5 Labs via the support contact listed on the extension's store page.
