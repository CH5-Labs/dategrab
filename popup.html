DateGrab

Welcome to DateGrab! Turn any webpage into clickable Google Calendar events in one click.

To get started, grab a free API key from Groq (ultra-fast, no credit card required):

  1. Open Groq Console and sign in with Google or GitHub.
  2. Click Create API Key and copy your key (starts with gsk_...).
  3. Paste your key below to start scanning pages.
🔒 Your API key is stored locally in your browser. When you scan a page, its text is sent to Groq to find dates.
🔒 Scanning sends this page's text and links to Groq's AI service to find dates. Nothing is sent until you click. Use "Change key" to remove your key and stop.
